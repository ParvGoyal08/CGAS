5. **API authentication details and rate limits.**
6. **Guidance on licensing:** what derived, non-raw annotations and benchmark artefacts we may store or share, and on what terms.
7. *(Optional)* Access to the Foodoscope flavour-compatibility / substitution endpoints, which we would use as baselines.

We will attribute every resource, follow the rate limits and licence/redistribution terms, keep raw third-party data private where required, and clearly separate source facts, model estimates and our own derived annotations.
