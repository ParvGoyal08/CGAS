## Answer in 5 lines

1. The closest precedent for our DEL-3 Gold is the **English Recipe Flow Graph corpus**: 300 recipes annotated with recipe named entities **and a flow graph** of steps, tools, ingredients and intermediate products. Inter-annotator agreement is **82.3–90.5 F1** [V].
2. **RISeC** annotates 260 SIMMR recipes as a DAG of entities and PropBank-style relations including **zero anaphora** (implicit arguments). Its annotators were "in close communication during the entire annotation process" [V]. That maximises consistency but does not measure *independent* agreement, and no agreement figure was found [U].
3. Our requirement (v4.0 AH6, AI4) is stricter than both: **independent double annotation, no LLM pre-fill, agreement reported before adjudication.**
4. Because our gold is **event-level** (operations on physical lots), not span-level, agreement must be computed on replayed graphs. Each annotator names entities freely, so IDs must be canonicalised by derivation before edges are compared.
5. **Decision enabled:** P4 guideline v0 with a YAML gold format that the WP-07 engine replays (structural validation for free), plus an ID-invariant agreement tool reporting event F1, relation-edge F1, merge/split F1, quantity agreement and state-field agreement.

## Findings

- [V] Yamakata Y., Mori S., Carroll J. (2020) *English Recipe Flow Graph Corpus.* LREC 2020, pp. 5187–5194. "The corpus consists of 300 recipes … inter-annotator agreement ranges from 82.3 to 90.5 F1 … our systems achieve 71.1 to 87.5 F1."
- [V] Jiang Y., Zaporojets K., Deleu J., Demeester T., Develder C. (2020) *Recipe Instruction Semantics Corpus (RISeC): Resolving Semantic Structure and Zero Anaphora in Recipes.* AACL-IJCNLP 2020. 260 SIMMR recipes; annotated in BRAT as a DAG (vertices = entity spans incl. actions and intermediate products; edges = relations); "Three expert annotators … in close communication during the entire annotation process"; data at github.com/YiweiJiang2015/RISeC.
- [C] ReciFine (Ibrahim, Ravikumar, Stevens, Batista-Navarro; Findings of EACL 2026): RecipeNLG extended with >97M entities of 10 types; releases a human-annotated benchmark. Agreement details not checked [U].

## Consensus | Contested | Emerging | Unknown

- **Consensus:** recipe semantics needs graph structure (intermediates, implicit arguments), not just spans. Agreement in the low-to-high 80s F1 is achievable for trained annotators.
- **Contested:** independent vs collaborative annotation. Collaboration raises consistency but hides ambiguity; v4.0 requires independence.
- **Unknown:** agreement levels for *state fields* and *quantity allocation*. No precedent was found, so we will be the first measurement for our own schema; no external target can be claimed.

## Implications for P4 (the guideline)

- **Proposed agreement gate:** report event F1, edge F1, merge/split F1, quantity agreement and state-field agreement *before* adjudication. **Revise the guideline if merge/split F1 < 0.80.** This uses the lower end of r-FG's range as a sanity anchor, not as an equivalent metric, and is to be confirmed by the humans.
- Agreement must be ID-invariant, so entities are canonicalised by derivation (concept + operation + parents).
- **Annotation tool v0:** plain YAML plus engine replay as the validator. Tool choice (BRAT, INCEpTION, Label Studio) is deferred to RS-07-06 and only revisited if YAML slows the pilot.
